# Origin Sculpture brand and site rules

## Brand position and reader

Write for architects, landscape designers, hospitality teams, developers, art consultants, and private buyers evaluating a substantial sculpture project. Assume many readers are not fabrication specialists. Present Origin Sculpture as a practical manufacturing and project partner. Let understandable choices, relevant site pages, material knowledge, samples, drawings, and verified project evidence carry authority.

Default public language: American English. Default planning/review language: Chinese.

## Voice

- Calm, precise, design-aware, and commercially useful.
- Premium without repeatedly saying `luxury`, `timeless`, `exceptional`, or `meaningful`.
- Concrete about setting, viewing distance, light, touch, weather, drainage, fabrication, finish, installation, and care.
- Honest about tradeoffs. A finish can improve an outcome but cannot rescue the wrong alloy, substrate, coating system, drainage detail, or maintenance plan.
- Invite consultation after giving the reader enough information to make progress independently.
- Translate technical language into customer consequences. State what the term means, why the customer should care, and what they should approve or ask next.
- For custom-process, ordering, service, and company topics, lead from Origin's current site structure and the customer's journey. Do not spend most of the article narrating internal production stages, quality acronyms, engineering coordination, or workshop controls.

## Site facts and canonical patterns

- Production domain: `https://originsculpture.com`
- Default blog: `/blogs/news`
- Product URLs: `/products/<handle>`
- Article URLs: `/blogs/news/<handle>`
- Canonical link source: the live Shopify sitemap, not search-result URLs or guessed handles.

Use the live sitemap because product handles and titles may contain legacy spelling or naming inconsistencies. Preserve the actual canonical URL even when the display anchor is corrected and natural.

## Published format baseline

The current live structural baseline for release 2.4.5 is `https://originsculpture.com/blogs/news/sculpture-finish-guide`, verified 2026-08-21. Preserve its theme-rendered H1/body hierarchy and clean Origin article rhythm while adapting sections to the new topic. The corresponding final-upload Word appearance is locked separately in `word-output.md` and `assets/format-reference/latest-format-page-1.png`.

The supplied 12 indexed examples generally use:

- one theme-rendered article title/H1;
- approximately 1,200–2,200 body words in the legacy set;
- several H2 decision sections with supporting H3s;
- four to eight images;
- tables for material, cost, or care comparisons;
- checklists and FAQ sections for buyer guides;
- a final project-oriented call to action.

Improve this baseline rather than copying it mechanically. New Pillar Blogs use 1,800–2,500 useful body words; new Supporting Blogs use 1,000–1,600. Include one evidence-backed, topic-specific Origin experience section at 10–20% of body words without turning it into a reusable brand pitch. The August 18, 2026 audit found zero product-body links in all 12 supplied articles and inconsistent article-to-article linking. New articles must close that gap with contextual links.

Use the site's hierarchy and editorial rhythm, not its legacy weaknesses. Preserve one theme-rendered H1, readable H2/H3 sections, useful tables/checklists, and a restrained final CTA. Improve evidence, answer speed, semantic coverage, contextual product proof, and internal-link distribution. Do not imitate competitor wording or manufacture project evidence.

## Business-claim policy

Do not infer a factory location, founding year, client name, installed project, staff credential, warranty, free shipping promise, engineering service, material grade, lead time, or price from generic industry practice. Use only current Origin Sculpture pages or user-provided evidence. Mark missing facts and block publication if the claim is necessary.

Treat sample/product pages as evidence of available visual options, not proof that a finish is suitable for every environment. Recommend a physical approved sample when color, gloss, patina, texture, or directionality matters.

## Known site-level issue to flag separately

The audited HTML on August 18, 2026 rendered `My Store` in the title suffix and Open Graph site name. This is a theme/store configuration issue, not something article copy can fix. Flag it once in site QA and recommend replacing it with `Origin Sculpture`; do not repeat it inside every article.
